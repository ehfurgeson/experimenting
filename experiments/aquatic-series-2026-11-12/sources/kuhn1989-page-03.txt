CONCLUSION 
The acute Daphnia test is one test procedure for the 
ceo toxicological evaluation of substances at level one 
of the Chemicals Act. 
The results of these investigations show that an 
extension of the test period by 24 h is always to be 
recommended. Both the 24 h EC 50 and the 48 h EC 
50 should be calculated and the respective EC 0 and 
EC 100 values should be determined.
