  • kobayashi1998 — pp.342–346; Figures 1–4. Full paper recovered; separate solution and soil protocols; graphical responses are not digitized.
  • melilotus2018 — Sections 2.1.1, 3.1.3, 3.2.1, 3.2.4–3.2.5. Isolation of salicylic acid and coumarin from Melilotus officinalis; spectral descriptions. Reported salicylic melting point needs independent chemistry review.
  • oregano2018 — GC/MS results; Table 1. Carvacrol, thymol and p-cymene identified in commercial oregano oil; isolated pure-compound characterization is not supplied.
  • spices2007 — Author abstract; original article pp.921 onward. GC-MS clove eugenol and oregano carvacrol/p-cymene. Full methods and source-standard identity pending.
  • walker2003 — Primary article abstract; DOI 10.1021/jf021166h. trans-Cinnamic acid among ten root-exudate compounds characterized by proton/carbon NMR; full spectra pending.
  • sarmentine2010 — Primary isolation abstract; DOI 10.1021/jf102087c. Bioassay-guided isolation from Piper longum fruits; complements isolation methods in Dayan 2015.
  • dayan2015 — Weed-spectrum methods; Tables 1–2; Figure 8. Methods specify 10 mg/mL; table headings specify 20 mg/mL. Preserve dose conflict; injury bands are not exact percentage values.
  • phenylpropionic2007 — Primary isolation abstract. 3-Phenylpropionic acid isolated from terrestrial Streptomycetes and characterized by IR/MS/proton/carbon NMR; microbial origin, not proof of plant origin.
  • hydroxyphenylacetic2025 — Primary abstract; compound 4. 2-Hydroxyphenylacetic acid reported isolated from Actinomycetospora strain CA287887; full source identity confirmation pending. Para-isomer isolation papers do not support this ortho isomer.
  • resorcylic_primary — Compound 5; source NMR/structure table. Primary isolation lead naming 2,4-dihydroxybenzoic acid; requires source-structure and spectral consistency review.
  • juglans2024 — Section 2.1.4; compound 141; experimental analysis. Juglans nigra chemical characterization; juglone annotation reported. Full isolation/standard identity evidence still to be checked.
  • rumen1964 — Summary, p.23. 3-Phenylpropionic acid isolation and benzoic acid chromatographic identification in rumen fluid; natural biological occurrence, not plant-specific origin.
  • cinnamon2005 — Chericoni et al., J Agric Food Chem 53:4762–4765; primary lead cited by Muñoz reference 48. Cinnamomum zeylanicum essential-oil study; exact trans-cinnamaldehyde characterization not verified. DOI/name locator must be confirmed.
