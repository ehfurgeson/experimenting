Decision: defer combined pathway C

Original-study and teacher-training independence are insufficient for an untouched experimental evaluation. No combined fit was run. B scores measure teacher reproduction, not experimental accuracy.

Per-fold logistic weights and OOB permutation diagnostics are retained. The binary whole-cohort OOB diagnostic is separate from scaffold validation. Its repeat SD is stability, not a p-value. No calibrated intervals, original-publication/provider holdouts, measured-feature cohort or qualified external plant-activity model are available.
