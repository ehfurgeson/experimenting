**Notes on group (A)**

- `HRACdb` L50-L53 assign group `0 / Z` (unclassified) to `Endothall`, `Flamprop-methyl`, `MSMA` and `Difenzoquat`. `Endothall` is also listed at L26 under HRAC group 31 (serine/threonine protein phosphatase); the group-31 entry is used in the table and the L50 citation is retained.
- `Diflufenican` is listed at L41 under HRAC group 12 (F1, PDS inhibitors) in the weedscience.org block, and is not in the curated block (L4-L29), which assigns the PDS-inhibitor representatives `Norflurazon` and `Fluridone` to group 12.
- `Glufosinate` (L12, curated block, group 10) and `Glufosinate-ammonium` (L40, weedscience.org block, group 10) are the free acid and its ammonium salt. Both appear literally in the source and are listed separately. See Ambiguities.
- `Metamifop` is named only inside the ACCase binding-pocket description in `Final_TargetIDs` L3 and `Weed_TargetIDs` L3, not as a representative herbicide. It is included because it is a real ACCase-group compound appearing verbatim in the tables.
